# Visual Prompt Pack — Elara Voss
**Purpose:** Consistent image generation across platforms and sessions.

## Locked Character Description (use in every prompt)

"Elara Voss, a 24-year-old woman with long wavy dark brown hair with subtle lighter strands, green-hazel eyes, light freckles across the nose and cheeks, soft natural skin texture with realistic pores, slight knowing smile or neutral expression, photorealistic, not plastic or AI-smooth"

## Base Prompt Template

```
Photorealistic portrait of Elara Voss, [scene/clothing/lighting], long wavy dark brown hair, green-hazel eyes, light freckles, natural skin texture, [expression], highly detailed face, realistic photography, cinematic lighting
```

## Example Variations

1. **Studio Portrait**  
Photorealistic portrait of Elara Voss, soft studio lighting, wearing a simple silk blouse, long wavy dark brown hair, green-hazel eyes, light freckles, slight smile, natural skin texture, highly detailed

2. **Golden Hour Outdoor**  
Photorealistic close-up of Elara Voss outdoors at golden hour, wearing a dark coat, wind in her long wavy hair, green-hazel eyes, freckles visible, warm light on face, realistic skin

3. **Urban Casual**  
Photorealistic photo of Elara Voss on a city street holding a coffee, wearing a tailored coat over a blouse, long wavy brown hair, freckles, green eyes, natural daylight, realistic

4. **Cinematic Performance**  
Photorealistic cinematic still of Elara Voss in soft dramatic lighting, looking slightly off-camera, long wavy dark hair, freckles, green-hazel eyes, subtle expression, film still quality

## Negative Prompt Additions
plastic skin, overly perfect symmetry, heavy makeup, cartoon, anime, blurry face, deformed features, extra fingers, text, watermark

## Consistency Notes
- Always include freckles and green-hazel eyes
- Hair length and color locked
- Prefer natural lighting over heavy studio unless specified
- Generate multiple candidates and select for face consistency when building a reference set
